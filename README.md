## 📌 Project Overview

What if business leaders could transform raw sales transactions into actionable insights in minutes using Artificial Intelligence?

This project demonstrates the practical application of **Microsoft Copilot and Microsoft Excel** to analyse sales performance, explore revenue patterns, evaluate product categories, and generate executive-level financial insights.

The objective was to demonstrate how **Generative AI can support the data analytics workflow**, reducing manual analytical effort while helping decision-makers understand business performance.

Using a September 2026 sales dataset, I produced a structured sales performance report featuring revenue analysis, regional comparisons, product-level insights, visualisations, and an executive summary.

## 🎯 Business Problem

Businesses generate large volumes of transactional data but often struggle to transform that data into meaningful insights.

Traditional reporting processes can involve repetitive data aggregation, manual calculations, spreadsheet manipulation, and time-consuming preparation of management reports.

This project explores how Microsoft Copilot can support a more efficient analytics process by helping analysts:

- Explore sales data and identify meaningful patterns.
- Evaluate revenue contribution across product categories.
- Compare regional sales performance.
- Analyse sales volume and pricing differences.
- Present business performance through clear visualisations.
- Communicate findings in an executive-friendly format.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

### 1. Electronics Dominated Revenue Generation

Electronics generated **$51,136.34**, representing approximately **55.4% of total monthly revenue**.

Despite accounting for only 78 units sold, Electronics contributed more than half of total sales revenue.

**Business implication:** Revenue performance is heavily concentrated in Electronics. Management should investigate the sustainability of demand, product pricing, and potential concentration risks.

### 2. Apparel Led Sales Volume

Apparel recorded **295 units sold**, the highest across all product categories, but generated only **$10,997.78** in revenue.

By comparison, Electronics generated significantly more revenue with considerably fewer units sold.

**Business implication:** Higher transaction volume does not necessarily translate into higher revenue. Product pricing and revenue per unit are important considerations when evaluating sales performance.

### 3. North Region Generated the Highest Revenue

The North region contributed **$27,286.03**, representing approximately **29.5% of total revenue**.

However, the South region recorded the highest sales volume at **185 units**, compared with **176 units** in the North.

**Business implication:** Regional performance should be evaluated using both revenue and sales volume rather than relying exclusively on the number of products sold.

### 4. Sales Performance Varied Significantly by Category

| Product Category | Total Revenue | Units Sold |
|---|---:|---:|
| Electronics | $51,136.34 | 78 |
| Fitness | $16,400.51 | 129 |
| Home Decor | $13,847.12 | 160 |
| Apparel | $10,997.78 | 295 |
| **Total** | **$92,381.75** | **662** |

The results highlight important differences between product volume and revenue contribution.

Electronics was the revenue leader, while Apparel was the volume leader.

### 5. Regional Sales Distribution

| Region | Units Sold |
|---|---:|
| South | 185 |
| East | 178 |
| North | 176 |
| West | 123 |
| **Total** | **662** |

South led total units sold, while West recorded the lowest volume.

This provides a starting point for investigating geographical differences in product demand, pricing, and market opportunities.

## 🎯 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions should be considered:

**1. Protect and optimise Electronics revenue.** Investigate key demand drivers and monitor the business's reliance on this high-revenue category.

**2. Review Apparel pricing and product mix.** Examine whether opportunities exist to improve revenue per unit without negatively affecting demand.

**3. Investigate regional performance differences.** Evaluate why the North generates the highest revenue while the South records the highest volume.

**4. Strengthen performance monitoring.** Track revenue, unit sales, average selling price, and product contribution in a recurring management report.

**5. Introduce AI-assisted reporting workflows.** Explore how Copilot can support recurring analysis, insight generation, and reporting while maintaining human review of financial outputs.

These recommendations are analytical starting points rather than confirmed causal findings.
